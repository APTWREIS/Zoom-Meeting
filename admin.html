Google Sheets Cloud Database Setup (1 Minute)

Google Sheets acts as a free, live online Excel database. Follow these 4 quick steps to connect your portal:

  1. Create a new Google Sheet:
    Go to sheets.new in your browser and name the spreadsheet "APTWREIS Zoom Meetings & Attendance".
  2. Open Apps Script Editor:
    In your Google Sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Paste the Backend Script:
    Delete whatever is in Code.gs, copy the script below, and paste it into the editor:
    Loading script...
  4. Deploy as Web App:
    • Click Deploy (top right blue button) → New deployment.
    • Click the gear icon ⚙️ next to "Select type" → choose Web app.
    • Execute as: Select Me.
    • Who has access: Select Anyone (CRITICAL: allows all officers/principals to load meetings and mark attendance without Google sign-in).
    • Click Deploy, grant permissions if prompted, and copy the Web app URL.
  5. Connect to Portal:
    Paste your Web app URL into the box in the Admin portal and click "Save & Connect".
